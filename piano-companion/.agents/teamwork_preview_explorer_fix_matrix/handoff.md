## 2. Logic Chain

1. **Deduction of Pianova Score & Demotion**:

   - *Discovery*: Active Apple App Store application *Pianova: Piano Lessons* by Valay Inc., Hailun Pianova digital pianos, and Pianova.com create severe likelihood of confusion and trademark collision (Observation 2).
   - *Adjustment*: BST is reduced by 5.0 points (10.0 -> 5.0).
   - *Recalculation*: `9.5 (KMJ) + 9.8 (EPH) + 9.4 (CCL) + 10.0 (UPH) + 5.0 (BST) = 43.7 / 50`.
   - *Ranking Impact*: With a total score of 43.7, Pianova falls below PianoNama (49.2), LoopKeys (47.4), KeyToon (46.9), and OctaPlay (46.2). Pianova drops from #2 to #5 among the finalists (Observation 4).

2. **Deduction of LoopKeys Elevation to #2 Runner-Up**:

   - *Comparison*: With Pianova demoted to 43.7, LoopKeys holds the second-highest total score in the entire pool (47.4), second only to PianoNama (49.2).
   - *Strategic Alignment*: LoopKeys highlights the core technical superpower of the application (A-B YouTube video measure looping) and is 100% free of app store copycat collisions.
   - *Conclusion*: LoopKeys is rightfully elevated to the official **#2 Runner-Up** and secondary feature-brand asset.
